Multiplot It: Composition and Trend of AI Affiliation

Published 15 October 2026. Portfolio Part II, Bundle II

For Module 10 I combined two related views of the AI affiliation data into one multiplot. A stacked bar chart sits on top of a line chart, and both are linked by a Counts/Share (%) toggle and a shared legend. It is built in Plotly and hosted on Posit Connect Cloud.

The Visualisation

Design Choices

Juxtaposition. Both charts use the same data with the same year on the x axis and the same colour for each affiliation. The bars show each year's total, and the lines show each affiliation on a common baseline.

Visual emphasis. The bar chart is on top as the main view. In the line chart the Industry line is thicker and solid while the other lines are thinner and dashed.

Interactive Features

Counts/Share (%) toggle. One button switches the y axis of both charts and the annotation between the number of systems and each affiliation's share of the year.

Shared legend. Clicking a category in the legend hides it in both charts at once, which filters the data and connects the two views.

Interactive Views

Data Card

Title Affiliation of researchers building notable AI systems by year of publication. Composition and trend
Summary Two related views of the notable AI systems introduced each year from 1950 to 2025. A stacked bar chart shows how each year's total is split by affiliation, and a line chart shows the trend of each affiliation on a common baseline. There were 34 systems in the 1990s, 324 in the 2010s and 543 from 2020 to 2025. Industry accounts for 97 of the 106 systems in 2025. Viewers can hover for exact figures and switch both charts between numbers and percentages.
Data Sources
  • Our World in Data. (2025, March 12). Affiliation of research teams building notable AI systems by year of publication. Our World in Data. https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/affiliation-researchers-building-artificial-intelligence-systems-all
  • Epoch AI. (2026, August). Data on AI models [Data set]. https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models?view=graph&tab=notable#explore-the-data
Mapping
  • X axis: years of publication, shared by both charts.
  • Y axis: counts or share view. Number or percentage of AI systems published that year.
  • Colour: affiliation category, the same colours in both charts. Collaboration is hatched in the bars.
  • Position: the stacked bar chart is on top as the main view, with the line chart below.
  • Line weight: Industry is a thicker solid line and the other categories are thinner and dashed.
  • Interaction: hovering bars and lines. The Counts/Share (%) buttons switch the y axis of both charts and the annotation, and the legend hides a category in both charts.
Important Notes
  • Category consolidation: Epoch AI documents six affiliation types (Industry, Academia, Research Collective, Non-profit, and two variants of collaboration). Our World in Data consolidated the two collaboration variants into one, and folded collective and non-profit into “Other”.
  • Missing years: 15 of the 76 years (e.g. 1951, 1953, 1956,...) have no notable systems. They were added as 0s.
  • Early years have very small counts. Shares before about 1990 swing between 0% and 100% in both charts.
  • Line chart uses the same data as the bar chart. In the share view each line is the category's percentage of that year's total.
